\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

We submit the regular paper ``Pure-Inertial Camera Motion Estimation from
Continuous IMU Streams'' for consideration in the \emph{IEEE Transactions on
Instrumentation and Measurement}.

The paper is a metrological characterization of inertial camera-motion
measurement: it defines the measurand, quantifies which part of a 3-s
displacement is observable from a continuous low-cost IMU stream and which
part requires an acquisition constraint, and compares the estimators under
one protocol. We provide a leakage-controlled reference and calibration chain
with an operational uncertainty budget and a simulated known-displacement
test, a digital twin with measured sensor parameters, and a compact
physics-structured IMU-only estimator with per-prediction uncertainty. The
experiments include protocol-matched reimplementations of public inertial
architectures, session-wise cross-validation, uncertainty coverage, and
translation trajectory error. Images are used for calibration, training
supervision and evaluation only; deployment inference is based solely on the
IMU stream. On synthetic data the proposed estimator lowers the error of the
strongest public architecture by 24\% with one seventh of its parameters; on
real data it matches that architecture within statistical resolution, and all
learned models clearly beat zero motion. We report this comparison as it
stands rather than claiming a significant accuracy win.

We make the following limitation explicit. The historical real-test recording
was excluded from gradient fitting and the initial fixed-split architecture
selection, but it was subsequently reused for reported exploratory
cross-validation-ensemble, stillness-gate and blend diagnostics. We therefore
call it ``held out'', not a one-shot sealed confirmatory test. Further
handheld sessions with a continuously visible board were not collected.
The effect of stop rate is therefore taken from a digital twin whose travel,
angular rate, noise and board-frame gravity match the existing recordings;
that twin does not replace the real estimator comparison.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, contains no
human-subject or clinical data, and has been approved by all authors. Software,
configuration, split manifests, the frozen study protocol and aggregate result
artefacts are available at
\url{https://github.com/liranyang123456-commits/EGO_Mo}; an archived release
with a DOI will be referenced in the final version.

The current IEEE two-column manuscript has 15 pages. We acknowledge the
mandatory overlength-page policy and will identify an author with financial
authority in PeerTrack.
